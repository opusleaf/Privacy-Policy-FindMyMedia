Privacy Policy

FindMyMedia

Effective date: 7 August 2026

Thank you for using FindMyMedia (operated by OpusLeaf). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes, how it is used, and your choices. FindMyMedia helps you find, browse, search, and clean WhatsApp-related media that is already stored on your device.

1. Summary

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • Your media files are processed on your device. We do not operate a server that stores your photos, videos, documents, or WhatsApp files.
  • We use Google AdMob for ads and Google Play Billing for optional Pro purchases.
  • This app is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

2. Information we process

2.1 On-device media and files

To provide core features (scan, gallery, search, status download, trash), the app reads media and related file metadata available on your device through Android APIs such as the MediaStore and, where needed, user-granted folder access (SAF). Processing (indexing, thumbnails, search text extraction, move-to-trash) happens locally. Files are not uploaded to OpusLeaf servers.

2.2 App preferences stored on device

The app stores small amounts of data locally (for example preferences, free-tier usage counters, Pro entitlement flags, and ad cooldown timers) using on-device storage such as SharedPreferences and a local database. This data stays on your device unless you clear app data or uninstall.

2.3 Optional feedback you send

If you use Send feedback, your email client may open with a message that includes device and troubleshooting details you choose to send (for example app version, Android version, and diagnostic notes). That content is only sent if you send the email.

2.4 Information we do not collect as an account

FindMyMedia does not create OpusLeaf user profiles, and we do not run a backend that stores your media library or purchase history on our servers. Purchase records are handled by Google Play under Google’s terms.

3. Permissions

Depending on your Android version, the app may request:

  • Photos / videos / audio (media) — to find and show WhatsApp-related media via MediaStore.
  • Storage (older Android) — limited legacy storage access where required.
  • Folder access (SAF) — optional, user-initiated access to folders such as WhatsApp Media for statuses or documents that MediaStore alone cannot fully expose.
  • Notifications — for scan or background work progress where applicable.
  • Advertising ID — used in connection with ads (see AdMob below).

FindMyMedia is designed without requiring “All files access” (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) for Play releases.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

Free users may see banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect or receive device and advertising identifiers and usage data related to ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention, according to Google’s policies.

  • AdMob / Google Privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data in ads products: https://business.safety.google/privacy/

Purchasing Pro is intended to remove ads while Pro is active, as described in the app.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional Pro plans (pro_monthly, pro_yearly, pro_lifetime) are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment and purchase data. OpusLeaf does not receive your full payment card details. Restore purchases uses Google Play’s purchase state on your device / Google account.

Google Play Terms: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited sharing occurs only as needed for:

  • Google AdMob (advertising on free tier)
  • Google Play (distribution, billing, license testing)
  • Feedback you voluntarily email to us
  • Content you choose to share via the system share sheet (e.g. app link or files you select)

7. Data retention and security

  • Media and local indexes remain on your device under your control.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes local app storage.
  • We use industry-standard practices appropriate for an on-device app; no method of storage is 100% secure.

8. Children’s privacy

FindMyMedia is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us via feedback email, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

9. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke media / folder permissions in Android settings (some features will stop working).
  • Reset advertising ID or limit ad tracking in device settings (where available).
  • Upgrade to Pro to remove ads while entitled.
  • Clear app data or uninstall to remove local app data.

10. International users

The app is designed primarily for on-device processing. Third parties such as Google may process data in other countries subject to their policies and applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised policy.
